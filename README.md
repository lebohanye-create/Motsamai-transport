## About

Motsamai Transport Services provides safe, reliable, and affordable passenger transport for short and long distances across Lesotho and South Africa. We service events, funerals, weddings, airport transfers and other long-distance trips with comfortable 7-seater vehicles.

## Services

- Passenger Transport (7 Seater)
- Lesotho & South Africa Trips
- Events, Funerals & Weddings
- Airport & Long Distance Travel

## Booking

To book a trip or request a quote, contact us using the phone number or WhatsApp link above. Please include:
- Pickup location
- Destination
- Date & time
- Number of passengers
- Any luggage or special requirements

## Example

For example, to request an airport transfer:
1. Send a WhatsApp message to 074 304 2676 (or click the WhatsApp link).
2. Provide pickup address, flight arrival time, and number of passengers.
3. We will confirm availability and fare.
